One line, in the voice of the Moon, replying at last to the wishes, howls and
late-night promises of everyone who has ever stared up at it.

Run `node main.ts` (Node 22+) and it writes the Moon's comeback to
`/output/entry.txt`.

Entry for the [make.fun competition "The Moon Finally Answers Back"](https://make.fun/competitions/u-mang-the-moon-finally-answers-back-781a7ec1).
